UCF-QNRF - A Large Crowd Counting Data Set
------------------------------------------

This archive contains 1535 images and their annotations used in the following paper:

H. Idrees, M. Tayyab, K. Athrey, D. Zhang, S. Al-Maddeed, N. Rajpoot, M. Shah, 
Composition Loss for Counting, Density Map Estimation and Localization in Dense Crowds, 
in Proceedings of IEEE European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV 2018), Munich, Germany, September 8-14, 2018.
